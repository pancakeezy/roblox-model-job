Roblox Model Jobs

Before you take a job

  • Agree on the price, the deadline and the number of revisions in writing first.
  • Ask for part of the money up front, or for payment after each model.
  • Send screenshots or a watermarked preview, and hand over the .blend / .fbx after you're paid.
  • Never download "test files" or run programs a client sends you.
  • Robux amounts show a rough dollar value using Roblox's DevEx rate. What you'd actually cash out depends on DevEx rules.
